      The barbell problem

      Your workforce has two extremes. Most tools only see one.

      Every team has both ends — people running on fumes, and people quietly coasting. TeamTrace plots every person on the barbell, flags employee burnout and disengagement early, and shows you which playbook fits.

      One end burns out

      Part of your team is running on fumes — too many meetings, weekend pings, context they cannot shake. The people doing the most work are the most at risk.

      The other end drifts

      Another part is invisible — quiet quitting, long lunches, focus blocks that never actually start. By the time you notice, they have already accepted another offer.

      Most tools only see one end

      Activity trackers punish the deep worker to catch the coasting one. Wellbeing apps protect the burned-out and ignore the disengaged. Both ends need different interventions.

          Frequently asked questions

          Something else on your mind? Contact us.

          Is employee monitoring legal in India?

          Generally, yes, when it’s transparent, serves a legitimate business purpose, and employees are properly informed. The DPDP Act 2023 governs how personal data is collected and used, and TeamTrace is DPDP compliant. Check local employment law and take legal advice when you write your monitoring policy.

          Will my team feel spied on?

          Not when it’s used the way it’s designed. Share what’s tracked and why, use the data to coach and balance workloads, and let good work speak for itself. Good people would rather be measured on output than judged on who looks busiest.

          How is TeamTrace different from other employee monitoring tools?

          Most tools are good at tracking individual productivity but can’t show how the organization performs as a whole, because project, resource, time and productivity data sit in separate platforms. TeamTrace runs all of it as one workforce management software platform, then adds insider risk controls and a built-in PSA, so you can follow an hour from the first plan to the final invoice.

          Does it include time and attendance software?

          Yes. Automatic time tracking, timesheets with approvals, clock in and out, shifts, overtime, and leave are all built in.
